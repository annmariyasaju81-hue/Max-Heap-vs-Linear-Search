COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS

Max Heap:
- Insertion: O(log n)
- Finding maximum: O(1)
- Building by repeated insertion: O(n log n)
- Space: O(n)

Linear Search:
- Finding maximum: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

For the given 8 scores:
- Max Heap maximum: 95 (root access; no comparison needed after construction)
- Linear Search maximum: 95 (7 comparisons)

Note:
For finding the maximum only once in a small unsorted list, linear search is simpler.
For continuously maintaining the highest score with repeated insertions and maximum
queries, a Max Heap is more suitable because insertion is O(log n) and maximum
retrieval is O(1).
